Zepto Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund if:

1. The wrong product was delivered.
2. The product is damaged.
3. The product is missing from the order.
4. The order was cancelled but payment was deducted.

Refund requests should be submitted through the Zepto app within 48 hours of delivery.

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
